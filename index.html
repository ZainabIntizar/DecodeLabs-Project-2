01 · Foundation

Content is the source of truth.

Responsive design is not about squeezing a desktop page into a phone. It is about letting content guide the layout.

01
≈

Like water, content flows.

Flexible containers, relative units and intentional breakpoints let a page adapt instead of break.

max-width: 1200px;
02
▦

Structure with Grid.

Use two-dimensional Grid for the macro layout: sections, columns and page regions.

repeat(auto-fit, minmax(...))
03
↔

Align with Flexbox.

Use one-dimensional Flexbox for navigation, cards, controls and component-level alignment.

display: flex;
